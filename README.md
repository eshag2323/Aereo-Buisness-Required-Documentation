 Aereo – Business Requirements Document (BRD)

Project Overview
This project involves the preparation of a Business Requirements Document (BRD) for Aereo. The document outlines business requirements, objectives, and expectations to establish a structured understanding of the business problem and its proposed solution.

Project Objectives
- Understand and define business requirements.
- Identify key business objectives and expectations.
- Structure requirements into a clear and organised document.
- Establish alignment between business needs and proposed solutions.
- Support effective communication among stakeholders.

 Document Contents
The BRD covers the relevant business requirements, scope, objectives, functional expectations, and other documentation elements included in the project.

 Skills Demonstrated
- Business Requirements Documentation (BRD)
- Requirement Analysis
- Business Understanding
- Structured Documentation
- Analytical and Problem-Solving Skills
- Stakeholder Requirement Understanding

 Project Deliverable
The complete Business Requirements Document is available in the uploaded Word file in this repository.
